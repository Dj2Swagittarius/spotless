**Description**

Turn a folder of music into your own streaming app. In this video I set up a Spotless server from scratch with
Docker: install Docker, download one file, point it at your music, start it, and walk through the setup wizard.
No coding, no subscription, and nothing disappears when a license runs out.

Spotless is free and open source. It runs on a NAS, an old PC, a mini PC or a Raspberry Pi, and gives you
playlists, liked songs, synced lyrics, listening stats, discovery, profiles for the whole family, and an
optional AI DJ. There's a desktop app, an Android app, and any Subsonic app works too.

- Docker Desktop (Windows / Mac): https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop/
